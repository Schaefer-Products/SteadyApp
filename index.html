This is a cycle two people create. You can only change your half, and that's the half this program covers.

What a day looks like

Each day takes 8 to 12 minutes: one short lesson, one exercise, and a one-minute note about what happened in real life.

Days unlock in order, so there's nothing to decide when you're anxious. If you miss a day, you pick up where you left off instead of starting over.

You don't need her to take part, and you don't need to talk to anyone. It's you and the work.

      Who this is for, and who it isn't

      This is for men whose partner has said the relationship might end, who want to stay, and who are willing to work on their own half.

      It isn't the right fit if any of these are true:

      • There has been physical violence, threats, or sexual coercion in the relationship.
      • She has told you she's afraid of you, or a protective order is in place.
      • You monitor her phone or location, control the money, or limit who she sees.
      • She has asked for no contact, or she has left and you want to get her back.

      You'll answer a short check before you start and again each week. If any of these apply, the program won't start and you'll be pointed to better help. If you're drinking heavily or struggling with depression, the program adds pointers to get help alongside it.

      If you're thinking about ending your life, call or text 988 (U.S.).

      If you're being hurt in your relationship, the National Domestic Violence Hotline is 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788. Men are abused too.

        Questions

        Will this make her stay?

        We can't promise that, and no honest program can. Changing your half of a pattern can change how hard conversations go. What she decides is up to her, and part of the program is learning to respect that.

        Is this therapy?

        No. It's education and daily practice. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything and it doesn't replace a therapist. If you'd like one, it can help you go in with a clearer picture of your own patterns.

        Does she have to do it too?

        No. The program covers only your half, so nothing depends on her taking part.

        What if I miss a day?

        You pick up where you left off. Days unlock in order, and nothing resets.

        Steady is an educational program. It is not therapy, counseling, or medical treatment, and it can't promise any outcome for your relationship. It is currently in pre-launch.

        In crisis? Call or text 988 (U.S.). Being hurt by a partner? National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788.
